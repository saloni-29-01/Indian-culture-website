Celebrating the Colors of India

India’s culture is one of the oldest and most diverse in the world. Each state has its unique art, food, dance, and traditions. Here are some of our most famous festivals and cultural highlights:

Diwali

The Festival of Lights symbolizes the victory of light over darkness and good over evil. Homes are decorated with diyas and rangoli.

Holi

Known as the Festival of Colors, Holi celebrates love and the arrival of spring with joy, colors, and music.

Onam

Onam is Kerala’s harvest festival, marked with floral decorations (Pookalam), feasts, and traditional dances like Thiruvathira.

Durga Puja

Celebrated mainly in West Bengal, Durga Puja honors Goddess Durga and features grand pandals, cultural shows, and music.

Pongal

Pongal is Tamil Nadu’s harvest festival, thanking the Sun God for prosperity and good harvests.

Bihu

Bihu is Assam’s cultural festival celebrated with traditional dance, music, and feasting marking the Assamese New Year.
