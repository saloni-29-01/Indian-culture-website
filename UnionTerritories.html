Union Territories of India

Discover the unique charm of India’s 8 Union Territories — from the icy peaks of Ladakh to the tropical beauty of Andaman & Nicobar Islands 🇮🇳

Delhi

Capital: New Delhi

The heart of India — where ancient history meets modern lifestyle. Famous for Red Fort, India Gate & Parliament House.

Chandigarh

Capital: Chandigarh

Known as 'The City Beautiful', Chandigarh is famous for its architecture and serene Sukhna Lake.

Ladakh

Capital: Leh

Land of high passes, monasteries and the majestic Pangong Lake — a paradise for adventure lovers.

Jammu and Kashmir

Capital: Srinagar

‘Heaven on Earth’ — famous for Dal Lake, snow-capped mountains, and Kashmiri traditions.

Puducherry

Capital: Puducherry

A French-style coastal town known for beaches, Auroville and colonial architecture.

Andaman & Nicobar Islands

Capital: Port Blair

A tropical paradise known for beaches, coral reefs and the historic Cellular Jail.

Lakshadweep

Capital: Kavaratti

Beautiful islands famous for clear blue water, white sand, and coral life.

Dadra & Nagar Haveli and Daman & Diu

Capital: Daman

Blend of Portuguese culture, beautiful beaches, and calm coastal charm.
